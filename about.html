About AuraZen Studio

AuraZen Studio is a creative digital studio specializing in premium audiovisual experiences for digital platforms.

The studio develops original ambient videos, instrumental music, cinematic environments and immersive content designed to support relaxation, focus, productivity, emotional comfort and wellbeing.

AuraZen Studio combines visual direction, original music, digital production and automation to create consistent, high-quality experiences for audiences around the world.

Our Creative Projects

RZA — Relax Zen Ambience

RZA is focused on elegant and cinematic ambience for relaxation, deep focus, study, work and sleep.

Aura Morning Cafe

Aura Morning Cafe creates warm and welcoming coffeehouse experiences centered on cozy mornings, smooth instrumental jazz, calm productivity and emotional comfort.

AuraZen Social Automation

AuraZen Social Automation is an internal content management and publishing integration used by AuraZen Studio to manage and distribute its original audiovisual content to authorized social media accounts.

The integration may connect with official platform services, including TikTok Login Kit and TikTok Content Posting API, to authenticate authorized accounts and support approved content publishing workflows.

AuraZen Social Automation is intended only for AuraZen Studio's authorized accounts and does not access or publish content to unauthorized accounts.
