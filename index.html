Privacy Policy — AuraZen Social Automation

Last updated: August 20, 2026

AuraZen Studio respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information associated with the use of AuraZen Social Automation.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed, how it is used, and the choices available to authorized users.

1. About AuraZen Studio

AuraZen Studio is a creative digital studio focused on premium audiovisual experiences, digital content production, ambient media, music, relaxation, focus, wellbeing and social media content.

AuraZen Social Automation is an internal content management and publishing integration developed to help AuraZen Studio manage and publish its own authorized social media content.

The integration is intended only for AuraZen Studio's own authorized accounts and does not access or publish content to unauthorized accounts.

2. Information We May Process

When an authorized TikTok account connects to AuraZen Social Automation, the integration may process limited information provided through TikTok's official APIs and authorization system.

This information may include:

  • Basic account and profile information permitted by the authorized scopes.
  • TikTok account identifiers and authorization-related identifiers.
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens required to maintain authorized access.
  • Video files, captions and content selected by AuraZen Studio for publication.
  • Publishing status and technical information related to content delivery.

AuraZen Studio does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through this integration.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed through AuraZen Social Automation is used only for legitimate operational purposes related to AuraZen Studio's authorized social media accounts.

These purposes include:

  • Authenticating authorized TikTok accounts.
  • Connecting AuraZen Studio to TikTok through official APIs.
  • Uploading and publishing authorized video content.
  • Managing publishing workflows and content status.
  • Maintaining the technical operation and security of the integration.
  • Diagnosing errors and improving the reliability of the automation.

4. TikTok Authorization

AuraZen Social Automation uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

Access is granted only after an authorized TikTok user explicitly approves the requested permissions through TikTok's authorization process.

AuraZen Studio does not request permissions beyond those necessary for the operation of the integration.

5. OAuth Tokens and Security

Authentication tokens provided through TikTok OAuth are used only to perform authorized operations on connected accounts.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect credentials and authorization information from unauthorized access, disclosure or misuse.

Tokens are not intentionally displayed publicly and are not sold or provided to third parties for advertising purposes.

6. Content Publishing

Videos, captions and related information are published only when selected or approved as part of AuraZen Studio's content workflow.

AuraZen Social Automation does not independently create posts on unauthorized TikTok accounts.

7. Third-Party Services

AuraZen Studio may rely on trusted third-party services necessary to operate its technical infrastructure and content workflows.

These services may include:

  • TikTok and its official developer APIs.
  • Cloud storage services used for authorized media files.
  • Automation and workflow infrastructure.
  • Website hosting, domain and technical infrastructure providers.

Each third-party service operates according to its own privacy policy and terms of service.

8. Data Sharing

AuraZen Studio does not sell personal information obtained through the TikTok integration.

Information may only be shared when necessary to operate the integration, comply with applicable legal obligations, protect the security of the service, or when required by a trusted service provider involved in the technical operation of the platform.

9. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the authorized integration, maintain the content publishing workflow, comply with legal requirements or resolve technical and security issues.

Authorization information that is no longer required may be deleted, invalidated or revoked.

10. Revoking Access

Authorized users may revoke TikTok access through their TikTok account settings where available.

AuraZen Studio may also disconnect an account or remove stored authorization credentials when the integration is no longer required.

11. Data Deletion Requests

Requests concerning access, correction or deletion of information associated with AuraZen Social Automation may be sent to AuraZen Studio.

Please include sufficient information to identify the relevant authorized account or integration request.

AuraZen Social Automation is an internal business integration and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. International Data Processing

Technical services used by AuraZen Studio may process information in different countries depending on the infrastructure and service providers involved.

AuraZen Studio takes reasonable measures to use trusted providers and protect information processed through its integrations.

AuraZen Studio may update this Privacy Policy when its services, integrations, legal obligations or technical processes change.

The most recent version will always be published on this page together with its last updated date.
